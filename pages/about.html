The Origin

Damietta has been Egypt's furniture capital for over 60 years. Our workshop began in a small garage with a single lathe and a dedication to proper joinery.

Today, we refuse to compromise. We do not use MDF where solid wood belongs. We do not rush finishes. We build furniture that your children will fight over.

The Craft

01. Timber Selection

Every plank is inspected for moisture content and grain structure. We source FSC-certified hardwoods.

02. Joinery

Mortise and tenon. Dovetails. Hand-cut structural integrity that survives moves and generations.

03. Finishing

Hand-applied natural oils that let the wood breathe and age gracefully, rather than trapping it in plastic lacquers.
